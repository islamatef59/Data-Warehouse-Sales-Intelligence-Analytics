# Data Warehouse Sales Intelligence & Analytics 

## Project Overview
This repository contains a A collection of T-SQL queries for analyzing sales data warehouse trends. T-SQL analytical engine designed to extract actionable business insights from an enterprise Sales Data Warehouse (Gold Layer). The analytics suite performs deep exploratory data analysis (EDA), measure aggregations, time-series analysis, revenue contribution share, and Year-over-Year (YoY) performance benchmarking across customer demographics, product categories, and sales performance channels.

---

## Architecture & Schema Overview
The analytical queries run against a refined **Gold Schema** consisting of star-schema dimension and fact tables:

* `gold.fact_sales`: Central transactional fact table holding transactional metrics (`sales_amount`, `quantity`, `price`, `order_date`, `order_number`).
* `gold.dim_customers`: Customer dimensional metadata (`customer_key`, `first_name`, `last_name`, `country`, `gender`, `bdate`).
* `gold.dim_products`: Product dimensional metadata (`product_key`, `product_name`, `category`, `subcategory`, `cost`).

---

## Core Analytical Capabilities

### 1. Data Exploration & Metadata Auditing (`database_exploration.sql`, `date_exploration.sql`, `dimention_exploaration.sql`)
* Inspects relational metadata via `INFORMATION_SCHEMA.COLUMNS` to verify column data types, character bounds, and nullability constraints.
* Tracks historical sales spans, calculating operational duration in months via `DATEDIFF()`.
* Evaluates customer demographic distributions, calculating age bounds relative to dynamic dates (`GETDATE()`).
* Profiles unique dimensional attributes (distinct geographic countries, product categories, and subcategories).

### 2. Business KPI Measure Aggregations (`measure_exploration.sql`)
* Aggregates total enterprise sales revenue, item volumes, average selling price, order counts, product counts, and customer coverage.
* Constructs a unified high-level executive KPI summary using `UNION ALL` pipelines for fast C-suite reporting.

### 3. Magnitude & Multi-Dimensional Segmentation (`magntiude_analysis.sql`)
* Measures total customer counts segmented by geographic regions and gender demographics.
* Evaluates product diversity and average unit manufacturing costs across product categories.
* Maps revenue generation to customer profiles, identifying top revenue-contributing accounts.
* Maps distribution of sold products across operating countries.

### 4. Part-to-Whole & Category Contribution Analysis (`part_to_whole.sql`)
* Utilizes Common Table Expressions (CTEs) and window aggregate functions (`SUM() OVER()`) to compute the proportional percentage share of revenue generated by each product category.

### 5. Advanced Ranking & Customer/Product Profiling (`ranking_analysis.sql`)
* Identifies top 5 and bottom 5 revenue-generating products using `TOP` clauses and `RANK() OVER()` window functions.
* Ranks top 10 high-value customers by gross revenue contribution and isolates low-engagement accounts by order counts.

### 6. Time-Series & Cumulative Trend Tracking (`over_time_analysis.sql`, `cumulative_analysis.sql`)
* Aggregates monthly performance using `YEAR()`, `MONTH()`, `DATETRUNC()`, and `FORMAT()` methods for flexible date grouping.
* Computes running revenue totals (`SUM() OVER(ORDER BY order_date)`) and multi-year cumulative moving average unit prices.

### 7. Performance Benchmarking & YoY Analysis (`performance_analsis.sql`)
* Uses window functions `LAG()` and `AVG() OVER(PARTITION BY ...)` to calculate Year-over-Year (YoY) sales variances.
* Categorizes product performance against historical product averages (`Above Average` vs. `Below Average`) and dynamic growth trends (`Increase in sales` vs. `Decrease in sales`).

## Sample SQL Highlight: Year-over-Year Performance Benchmarking

```sql
WITH yearly_product_sales AS (
    SELECT
        YEAR(f.order_date) AS order_year,
        p.product_name,
        SUM(f.sales_amount) AS current_sales
    FROM gold.fact_sales f
    LEFT JOIN gold.dim_products p
        ON f.product_key = p.product_key
    WHERE f.order_date IS NOT NULL
    GROUP BY YEAR(f.order_date), p.product_name
)
SELECT 
    order_year,
    product_name,
    current_sales,
    COALESCE(LAG(current_sales) OVER(PARTITION BY product_name ORDER BY order_year), 0) AS Previous_sales,
    AVG(current_sales) OVER(PARTITION BY product_name) AS Avg_sales,
    CASE 
        WHEN current_sales > AVG(current_sales) OVER(PARTITION BY product_name) THEN 'Above Average'
        WHEN current_sales < AVG(current_sales) OVER(PARTITION BY product_name) THEN 'Below Average'
        ELSE 'Avg'
    END AS Avg_change
FROM yearly_product_sales
ORDER BY product_name, order_year;
```
